**Background:**

* We are evaluating CDnow, an online music retailer where customers can order music CDs to be sent to them. They are seeking cohort based financing for future cohorts, where:  
  * We would provide them with a fixed amount of capital on a loan basis, fixed to a certain cohort of users (e.g. all users who have purchased their first CD in the 1997Q2).   
  * They will use this fixed amount of capital for marketing, advertising, and other distribution related costs.   
  * 50% of the revenue from that cohort is then repaid to us, until a cap of 1.2x of the original capital has been returned, after which CDnow has no further obligation.   
* To support us in our investment decision, they have sent us a dataset containing the entire purchase history up to the end of June 1998 of the cohort of 23,570 individuals who made their first-ever purchase at CDNOW in the first quarter of 1997\.  
  * Access the dataset: [https\://www\.brucehardie.com/datasets/CDNOW\_master.zip](https://www.brucehardie.com/datasets/CDNOW_master.zip)

**Output:**

* Propose an investment decision on:  
  * The “quality” of CDnow’s revenue \- i.e. the probability that CDnow’s revenue will continue into the future  
  * A reasonable forecast of revenue of CDnow’s future cohorts  
  * Whether you would invest, and how much you would provide for a single quarterly cohort  
* Highlight your assumptions and highlight where you would seek additional information from the business.
